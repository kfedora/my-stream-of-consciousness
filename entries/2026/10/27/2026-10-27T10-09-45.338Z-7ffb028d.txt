As far as I remember, the exact mechanism isn't explained in the movie - whether it is biological or electronic.

This is my take from what was said. No one is generating time, in the same sense as the space-time continuum. Genetic engineering has reached such a level at this point in the future that people no longer die naturally from disease or old age. So they all have some kind of kill switch in their body from birth. The kill switch is irrevocably tied to whatever genetic manipulation also allows people to stop aging past 25. When condition X is met, the kill switch activates. X is the internal counter hitting zero. The internal counter is no different to a prepaid credit card or a travel pass, except the credit ticks away 1 unit per second. There is a central bank that holds trillions of units of credit, which probably resembles a gigantic cryptocurrency wallet.

So no one is generating or exchanging 'time'. It's just a token that interacts with the body's kill switch. The same way your watch doesn't generate or receive time. It just counts units of time passing and displays the information.

Yeah. Everyone except the wealthy is living on a small amount of time credits that they earn for work. And you see them send one another units of time the sale you or I would lend someone money. They all just have an internal bank account that is always depleting and kills you if the balance goes to zero.

The overarching metaphor is that the super rich control the economy and continuously move the goalposts so that the right amount of people stay poor enough to keep working their asses off. In the world of the movie the system is constantly adjusted so that the optimal number of people die from running out of time each year, keeping the population from growing too much.
